The woven loafer

Mũthuri

Mũthuri is the loafer with the woven front. A panel of woven leather runs across the top of the shoe, with a smooth strap over it and plain leather around the rest. The sole is thick, which gives the shoe a solid look.

It is the more understated of the two styles, and the one Iratu started with.

Behind Iratu

It started with a pair of shoes.

A shoemaker in Kenya made a pair of leather shoes for me. I was impressed by how well they were made, and he can make more to order, so I decided to build a small brand around them.

Iratu is the Gĩkũyũ word for shoes. We are starting with two styles, Mũthuri and Mũgendi, each in brown or black. Because every pair is made to order, we make what has been ordered.

The photos on this site are reference images for now. We will replace them with photographs of Iratu's own pairs.

Questions

Sizing

Sizes run from EU 39 to 45. Choose your usual EU size in the order form. If you are between sizes or unsure, say so in the notes and we will advise before you pay.

Making time

Around five days is the current estimate. We confirm the making time with you before you pay. It does not include delivery.

Payment

Each pair is KSh 4,000. After you send the request, we reply on WhatsApp with payment details. Nothing is charged through this website.

Delivery

Delivery is charged separately if applicable. We confirm the cost and timing with you directly, based on where you are, and we do not give a delivery date until the making time is confirmed.

Fit and exchanges

We have not published an exchange policy yet. Because each pair is made to order, please ask any fit questions before you pay. We will tell you the exchange terms for your order before payment.
