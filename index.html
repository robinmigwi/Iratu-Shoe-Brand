A STORY THAT STARTS CLOSE TO HOME

A good pair
can change
the way you see.

Iratu started with a pair of shoes made by a local artisan. The quality, the detail and the care in the work prompted a simple question: why shouldn't footwear made here stand confidently alongside the brands we admire elsewhere?

We are starting small, with a maker whose work we believe in and two designs we want to put into the world. Each order supports local craft and gives us a chance to get the details right, pair by pair.

BEFORE YOU ORDER

Good to
know.

Buying online should feel clear. Here is what to expect when ordering a made to order pair from Iratu.

How long does a pair take to make?+

Making time is currently estimated at around five days. We will confirm the actual production window with the maker before we accept your order. Delivery time is separate and depends on your location.

Which sizes are available?+

The first collection is planned in EU sizes 39–45. We will help confirm your size before production. If you are between sizes or unsure about fit, tell us before paying so we can advise based on the maker's sizing.

What is the price and how do I pay?+

Each style is KSh 4,000 per pair, before any delivery charge. We confirm the order, payment details and delivery cost with you directly before production begins.

Can I exchange a pair if it does not fit?+

Please ask us about fit before placing the order. Our final exchange and fault policy will be shared clearly before payment. Do not assume a made to order pair is exchangeable unless we have confirmed the terms with you.

Where do you deliver?+

Delivery options and costs will be confirmed when you place your order. We will not promise a delivery date until the production window and courier timing are clear.
