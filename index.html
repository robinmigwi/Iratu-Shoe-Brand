About

Iratu is the Gĩkũyũ word for shoes.

The brand started with a pair of leather loafers made for the founder by a shoemaker in Kenya. The quality was good enough to build a small label around, so we are starting with two styles, each in brown and black, made to order.

Questions

Sizing

Sizes run from EU 39 to 45. Choose your usual EU size in the order form. If you are between sizes or unsure, say so in the notes and we will advise before you pay.

Making time

Around five days is the current estimate. We confirm the making time with you before you pay. It does not include delivery.

Payment

Each pair is KSh 4,000. After you send the request, we reply on WhatsApp with payment details. Nothing is charged through this website.

Delivery

We confirm delivery cost and timing with you directly, based on where you are. We do not give a delivery date until the making time is confirmed.
